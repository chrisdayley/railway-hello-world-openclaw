# v27 furniture atlas

Method: built-in image-generation tool. Final project asset: `assets/interior-furniture-v27.png`. Original generated file is retained. Canvas consumes cells directly; no external image editor or runtime service was used.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production GAME FURNITURE SPRITE ATLAS, transparent PNG. Make a perfectly regular 4 by 4 grid of 16 standalone richly detailed warm high-fantasy pixel-art furniture sprites, all viewed from identical top-down three-quarter RPG camera looking from south (north edge/back high, south edge/front low, front faces horizontal, not diamond-isometric). Square 1536x1536 sheet, each sprite strictly confined to one equal 384x384 cell, centered with at least 24px transparent margin. Genuine transparent background around every object, no opaque square backgrounds, no borders, no labels, no UI, no characters, no watermark. Each sprite should occupy most of its cell, use same scale/art direction and precise pixel clusters, beautifully textured wood, bronze trim, warm lantern highlights, little storytelling details. Exactly these positions row-major: Row1 col1 cozy carved oak bed with teal blanket/pillow; col2 tall stocked wooden bookshelf with books and jars; col3 stone fireplace burning orange with brass kettle; col4 merchant wooden counter with scales coins and folded fabrics. Row2 col1 blacksmith forge/anvil workbench with tools; col2 alchemist herb-and-potion worktable with glass bottles and mortar; col3 stack of shipping crates with ropes and folded canvas; col4 horse stall with visible hay and a saddled chestnut horse behind wooden railing. Row3 col1 wooden weaving loom with red patterned cloth; col2 group of three coopered wooden barrels; col3 canvas grain sacks and produce baskets; col4 rectangular oriental-style teal/gold rug laid flat. Row4 col1 wooden dining table with bread plates and candles; col2 indoor orchard planter with two dwarf fruit trees; col3 carpenter assembly bench with gears blueprints and tools; col4 tall glass-front potion cabinet, many glowing colored bottles. Important pixel-art environment asset style, sophisticated high-end handcrafted RPG art, not flat icons, not photographs. Keep every entire object visible within its own grid cell so code can draw cells directly.

Actual generated atlas is 1254×1254; the renderer derives cell dimensions from the loaded image rather than assuming the requested size.
