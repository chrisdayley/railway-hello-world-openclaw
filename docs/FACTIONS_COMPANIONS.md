# Everlight — Factions & Companions

## Faction philosophy
Factions are major parallel progression paths. Each has ranks, substantial questlines, unique rewards, vendors, headquarters, economic effects, and at least one late-game commitment point. Early membership can coexist, but certain rivalries eventually force choices.

## 1. Ironbound Wardens
Identity: monster hunters, road protectors, disciplined fighters.
HQ: Bastion Hallow in Cinderfen March.
Ranks: Recruit → Shieldbearer → Hunt-Captain → Warden-Marshal → First Warden.
Rewards:
- counter/parry skills,
- heavy armor sets,
- boss-hunting contracts,
- Warden Warhorse mount,
- unique Legendary greatblade **Roadkeeper**,
- late perk: bonus damage against elite/boss enemies after perfect defense.
Economic effects: military contracts can boost smithies, stables, and mines.
Rivalry: conflicts strongly with the Ashen Synod late in the game.

## 2. Veiled Archive
Identity: relic scholars, ruin explorers, cartographers, magical historians.
HQ: The Lantern Library beneath Starrest.
Ranks: Finder → Seeker → Curator → Lantern Adept → Grand Archivist.
Rewards:
- treasure-sense pulses,
- relic identification bonuses,
- map clues for secret rooms,
- rare magical gear,
- hidden Waystone network,
- unique Mythic relic **Eye of the Unwritten Path**.
Economic effects: antiquities and magical businesses become more profitable.
Rivalry: conflicts with the Gilded Hand over artifact ownership and black-market trade.

## 3. Gilded Hand
Identity: merchants, smugglers, financiers, information brokers.
HQ: The Counting House in Lumenport.
Ranks: Runner → Factor → Broker → Master of Coin → Gilded Regent.
Rewards:
- better buy/sell prices,
- exclusive land auctions,
- black-market Legendary stock,
- improved property intelligence,
- caravan fast-travel routes,
- unique ability **Leverage**: spend gold for temporary combat/economy buffs,
- late perk: premium business specialization.
Economic effects: strongest direct property and trade bonuses.
Rivalry: conflicts with the Veiled Archive and certain lawful town governments.

## 4. Ashen Synod
Identity: dangerous magic researchers, battlefield mages, forbidden ritualists.
HQ: The Ember Cloister in Gloamwood.
Ranks: Initiate → Cinder Adept → Flamebound → Ash Magister → Synod Voice.
Rewards:
- new spell branches,
- status builds,
- self-buff / risk-reward magic,
- enchanted armor,
- unique mount **Cinderwing Drake** late game,
- unique Mythic catalyst **Heartcoal Prism**.
Economic effects: magical conservatories and apothecaries gain event bonuses.
Rivalry: conflicts with Ironbound Wardens.

## 5. Free Roads Compact
Identity: explorers, caravaners, builders, pilots, independent adventurers.
HQ: mobile caravan city **Wayfarer’s Rest**.
Ranks: Trailhand → Pathfinder → Routekeeper → Roadmaster → Free Captain.
Rewards:
- mount speed and stamina,
- cheaper vehicle upgrades,
- improved fast travel,
- airship engineering shortcuts,
- unique utility gear,
- late-game airship module **Stormsail**.
Economic effects: caravans and logistics businesses improve.
Rivalry: generally neutral; can be completed alongside most factions but loses some exclusive rewards if the player makes authoritarian faction choices.

## Companion system
The player can recruit several companions but actively travels with one at a time initially. Late-game upgrades may allow a second support companion.

Companions gain Bond through travel, quests, combat, gifts, and personal story choices. Bond unlocks passive perks, combat abilities, camp conversations, and personal quests. Companions are not permanently killed by ordinary combat.

## Mira Vale
Role: agile archer/scout.
Personality: warm, observant, lightly sarcastic, curious about ruins but distrustful of institutions.
Combat: ranged pressure, marks targets, interrupt shot.
Bond rewards:
- improved crit windows,
- secret-path hints,
- high bond: **Twin Mark**, periodically marks two enemies for bonus damage.
Personal quest: uncover what happened to Mira’s missing survey team beneath Moonfall.

## Garran Holt
Role: shield fighter/tank.
Personality: practical, dry humor, former Warden who dislikes bureaucracy.
Combat: taunts, guard intercept, stun bash.
Bond rewards: survivability and defensive counter windows.
Personal quest: choose whether to expose or protect a disgraced Warden captain.

## Seli Aranor
Role: mage/support.
Personality: brilliant, absent-minded, enthusiastic, occasionally reckless.
Combat: elemental bolts, barriers, status cleansing.
Bond rewards: mana regeneration and spell amplification.
Personal quest: recover three lost pages from a spellbook that should not exist.

## Torren Pike
Role: rogue/alchemist.
Personality: charming opportunist, entrepreneurial, always evaluating value.
Combat: bombs, poison, traps, item efficiency.
Bond rewards: better consumables and loot conversion.
Personal quest: build or dismantle his old smuggling network.

## Nym
Role: mysterious nonhuman relic-bound companion.
Personality: literal, curious, slowly learning humor and emotion.
Combat: floating arcane construct; beams, shields, anomaly detection.
Bond rewards: Everlight interactions, hidden machinery access.
Personal quest: discover whether Nym is a person, tool, or fragment of the ancient network.

## Faction/companion interactions
- Companions react to major faction choices.
- Some can leave temporarily if the player commits to a faction they strongly oppose, but permanent loss should only come from explicit story choices.
- Certain companions unlock unique solutions to quests, but no main-story victory requires one specific companion.
