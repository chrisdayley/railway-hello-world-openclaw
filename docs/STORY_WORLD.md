# Everlight: Vale of Echoes — Story & World Bible

## Core premise
The world of Aeralis was once linked by luminous ley-roads called the Everlight Ways. They powered old skyships, warded cities, and connected distant sanctuaries. Generations ago, the Ways fractured during an event remembered as the Sundering. Most people now treat the surviving ruins as curiosities, hazards, or sources of salvage.

The player begins in Greenwake Vale during a local disturbance: ancient stone markers have begun humming again, monsters are behaving strangely, and a sealed ruin beneath Moonfall wakes for the first time in centuries. The player is not a chosen one by birth. Their importance comes from what they choose to do with access to the reawakening network.

The central mystery is that the Everlight is not simply returning. Someone is deliberately forcing dormant nodes awake, causing regions to become unstable. The apparent villain, Lord Vael Corven, argues that restoring the network is the only way to prevent Aeralis from slowly fragmenting into isolated, declining kingdoms. His methods are dangerous, coercive, and increasingly destructive, but his underlying problem is real.

## Main story structure
### Act I — The Vale Wakes (roughly 6–8 hours)
- Greenwake Vale tutorial/exploration region.
- Briarwatch becomes the first major hub.
- Recruit Mira.
- Discover that Moonfall Ruins are active.
- Defeat the Hollow Warden.
- Recover the first Aether Key fragment.
- Choose or postpone initial faction allegiance.
- First property purchases and first mount become available.
- End beat: a distant mountain beacon ignites, proving the event is not local.

### Act II — Roads of the Broken Kingdoms (roughly 12–15 hours)
The player gains freedom to tackle three large regions in flexible order.

1. **Cinderfen March**
   - Flooded causeways, ember-swamps, ruined keeps.
   - Themes: old grudges, mercenaries, trade routes, survival.
   - Major dungeon: The Drowned Forge.
   - Boss: Bellowsaint Rusk, an armored salamander-beast inhabiting a collapsed forge.
   - Reward path: heavy crafting, ore economy, armored mount upgrades.

2. **Sunmere Coast**
   - Bright coastal towns, sea caves, cliff roads, merchant islands.
   - Themes: prosperity, smuggling, political influence, opportunity.
   - Major dungeon: The Tidemirror Vault.
   - Boss: The Pale Leviathan’s Eye, a mobile arena encounter across broken platforms.
   - Reward path: trade economy, boats, rare merchants, treasure maps.

3. **High Aster Range**
   - Alpine villages, glacial caverns, ancient observatories.
   - Themes: discovery, old knowledge, isolation.
   - Major dungeon: The Starwell Observatory.
   - Boss: Orryx the Skybound, a winged stone guardian.
   - Reward path: advanced magic, airship components, early gliding/flying utility.

Act II ends when the player learns Corven is using restored Everlight nodes to rebuild a continent-spanning control lattice. The lattice can stabilize the world, but it can also let its operator suppress travel, power, and communications across entire regions.

### Act III — The Crown of Roads (roughly 8–10 hours)
- Political and faction conflicts intensify.
- Rival factions demand commitment on key resources.
- The player restores or seizes three Grand Waystations.
- Airship becomes fully functional.
- Flight opens floating ruins, isolated peaks, remote islands, and secret bosses.
- Corven captures the central Meridian Spire.
- The player chooses how much faction, economic, companion, and relic support to bring into the finale.

### Finale — Meridian Spire
The final boss is not a single-solution puzzle. It is a multi-phase mastery fight designed for melee, ranged, magic, companion-heavy, defensive, and hybrid builds.

Phase 1: Corven duels the player with adaptive weapons and arena movement.
Phase 2: the Spire destabilizes; hazards and summoned constructs create pressure but do not require a specific counter.
Phase 3: Corven binds himself to the lattice, gaining powerful but readable attacks.
Phase 4: optional if the player has discovered the hidden truth of the Ways — the player can confront the lattice’s ancient guardian intelligence. This is a harder post-story-capable variant rather than a mandatory gimmick route.

Victory depends on pattern recognition, execution, build quality, resource management, and optional preparation—not one required spell or item.

## Postgame
After the main ending, the world enters a new state with:
- empowered regional bosses,
- new Mythic world events,
- sealed Everlight chambers,
- faction epilogues,
- landmark property opportunities,
- ultimate airship upgrades,
- the optional superboss **Aurelion, First Keeper of the Ways**.

## Regions
### Greenwake Vale
Warm fields, forests, waterfalls, crumbled watchtowers, beginner secrets.
Hub: Briarwatch.

### Moonfall Ruins
Broken ceremonial city buried beneath silver-leaf woods. Dense secret passages and relic puzzles.

### Cinderfen March
Muddy war roads, blackwater swamps, red ember vents, fortress towns.
Hub: Ashbridge.

### Sunmere Coast
Golden cliffs, turquoise coves, merchant plazas, sea caverns.
Hub: Lumenport.

### High Aster Range
Snowfields, sky bridges, alpine meadows, observatories.
Hub: Starrest.

### Gloamwood
Optional darker forest region unlocked midgame. Home to rare beasts, witch circles, and hidden companion quests.

### Crownstep Isles
Late-game island chain accessible by upgraded boat or airship. Excellent treasure and property opportunities.

### Skyreach Expanse
Late-game aerial region of floating ruins and storm corridors.

### Meridian Basin
Final region surrounding the Spire; faction conflict, elite enemies, and endgame vendors.

## Exploration principles
- Every region contains visible-but-unreachable rewards that become accessible with later mounts, traversal abilities, or flight.
- Secret walls, odd tile patterns, mismatched statues, sound cues, and unusual enemy placement signal hidden content.
- Fast travel is generous once a location is discovered.
- Long traversal is for discovery; repeated traversal is minimized.
- Optional dungeons must offer meaningful rewards: unique gear, companions, abilities, property deeds, airship parts, or rare crafting resources.
