# Everlight — Progression, Loot, Crafting & Endgame

## Player progression
Target level cap at launch: 40, with postgame mastery beyond level cap through gear, faction ranks, relics, companions, economy, and airship upgrades.

Each level grants core stat growth plus periodic skill points. Starting specialties bias the opening only; no class lock-in.

## Combat paths
### Blade / melee
- light combo
- heavy attack
- guard / parry options
- dash attacks
- bleed, stagger, counter builds

### Ranged
- bows, hand-crossbows, thrown relics
- charged shots
- mobility and crit builds
- elemental arrows / crafted ammunition

### Arcane
- bolts, zones, barriers, summons, status magic
- mana management
- elemental and utility paths

### Hybrid
Gear affixes and relics deliberately support mixed builds.

## Equipment slots
- Weapon
- Offhand / focus
- Head
- Body
- Boots
- 2 Accessories
- Relic

## Rarity
Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic

Rarity raises affix budget and special-effect potential, but unique lower-rarity items can remain useful through upgrades.

## Gear upgrades
- Enhancement levels +0 to +10.
- +1–5 use common materials and gold.
- +6–8 use elite drops and regional materials.
- +9–10 require boss/event materials.
- Upgrading improves base stats; it does not reroll identity.
- Reforging changes one affix at increasing cost.
- Infusion lets a favorite weapon inherit selected late-game traits without becoming best-in-slot automatically.

## Loot table philosophy
Normal enemies: materials, gold, Common–Rare gear, tiny Epic chance.
Elites: improved Rare/Epic rates, elite-specific components.
Named bosses: guaranteed unique material plus meaningful chance at named equipment.
World-event bosses: boosted XP and special temporary drop table.
Secret bosses: hand-authored unique gear/relics plus high Legendary/Mythic odds.

## Crafting
Crafting complements drops rather than replacing them.
Stations:
- Smithy: weapons/armor/enhancement
- Alchemy bench: potions, bombs, coatings
- Relic table: accessory/relic tuning
- Stable/garage: mount and vehicle upgrades
- Sky dock: airship modules

Property ownership can improve stations or reduce costs.

## Mounts and vehicles
### Windstrider
Early fast land mount. Upgrade speed, sprint stamina, and rough-terrain handling.

### Warden Warhorse
Faction mount; armored, durable, charge utility.

### Marshrunner
Cinderfen mount; crosses mud/shallow water efficiently.

### Sunmere Skiff
Small boat for coast/islands; cargo upgrades and treasure sonar.

### Cinderwing Drake
High-tier faction flying mount with limited endurance; not a replacement for the airship.

### Everlight Airship
Major late-game progression system.
Modules:
- engine
- hull
- cargo hold
- workshop
- map room
- Stormsail
- relic scanner
- combat ward

Airship unlocks free traversal over most world barriers and access to aerial content.

## Airship construction milestones
1. recover Aether Core
2. acquire hull deed / salvage frame
3. recruit an engineer
4. obtain three regional components
5. resolve faction claim over the sky dock
6. maiden flight quest
7. late-game module progression

## Endgame boss principles
- No immunity puzzle requiring exact gear.
- Every major attack has multiple responses: dodge, block, interrupt, outrange, tank with mitigation, companion support, or positioning.
- Builds may have favorable matchups, never exclusive solutions.
- Optional preparation reduces difficulty through better gear, extra potion capacity, faction techniques, companion abilities, and relics.
- Final boss target: difficult enough to require repeated attempts for many players.

## Postgame loop
- Mythic world events become more frequent.
- Corrupted/empowered variants of regional bosses.
- sealed Everlight vaults.
- high-tier property auctions and landmark developments.
- faction epilogue quests.
- airship-only hidden regions.
- superboss chain ending with Aurelion, First Keeper of the Ways.

## Superboss: Aurelion
Four-phase fight with adaptive but readable patterns. Designed to challenge maxed builds without requiring a single counter. Rewards a unique Mythic relic and cosmetic airship aura plus prestige achievement, not mandatory story power.
