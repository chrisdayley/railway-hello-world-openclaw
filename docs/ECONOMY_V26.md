# Estates & Enterprises — v26

## Playable scope

Open **Menu → Ledger → Property market**. Northford starts with six deeds: the Mooncup Inn, Greenbottle Apothecary, Ember & Anvil, Bellkeeper's House, Windstrider Stable and Pella's Provisioners. The Lantern Road Parcel is a seventh opportunity. Individual deeds are also accessible in ordinary buildings; the market only exposes discovered regions.

The catalog contains 295 opportunities: seven in Northford, 168 individual homes/businesses across 24 atlas settlements, 24 additional development parcels, and the 96 existing regional investments. The Guildhall and protected dungeon structures remain civic/adventure locations, not purchasable buildings. Purchasing a home preserves its residents, quests and treasure.

## Develop and operate

Buy land, then choose an orchard, inn, smithy, apothecary, workshop, stable or warehouse. Development has a separate gold cost. A completed development creates a collidable building, working door, interior manager and property ledger. An inn provides rest, a stable provides mount services, and other businesses provide trade. Choices are permanent for this milestone; there is no demolition or redevelopment action.

Operating properties have three investment tracks: quality, capacity and security, each capped at rank three. Gold costs rise with rank. Revenue benefits are bounded, property values are capped relative to invested capital, and operating expenses grow with upgrades. Security reduces—not eliminates—bad incidents. Suitable businesses provide small, capped portfolio synergies.

Daily reports include revenue, costs, profit and the local business incident. Regional suitability, active/completed world events, faction affiliation, portfolio synergies and upgrades modify returns. Some days lose money. Profits or losses flow into one central treasury; transferring a positive balance moves spendable gold into the purse. The display distinguishes invested property value from liquid savings.

Days advance after five minutes of active, unpaused play, when resting, or using Advance day in the ledger. Nothing accrues from a real-world clock while the game is closed. Reports are deterministic by property and adventure day; the same day cannot settle twice, and reloading does not reroll it.

Owning an operating smithy, apothecary or stable reduces relevant item/mount prices. Better quality improves that discount, with a hard cap. These are implemented price effects, not promised future perks.

## Persistence and limits

Save schema 8 keeps equipment, progression, discoveries, existing property balances and history. Legacy named investments are reconciled with the individual building register; unknown legacy properties must remain manageable. Existing regional investments keep their IDs.

This is a playable economic foundation, not a complete Fable-scale management simulation. It does not yet model individual employees, supply-chain inventories, player-selected product pricing, business renaming, property sales or rent negotiation. No real-money purchases, online services or real-time waiting are required.
